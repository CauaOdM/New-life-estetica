# Project Brief: New Life Estética Landing Page

## 1. Project Overview
**Client:** New Life Estética  
**Location:** Blumenau, SC - Brazil  
**Objective:** Create a premium, high-conversion landing page that reflects the clinic's 20-year legacy of excellence and the personal touch of founders Nubia and Nuzi. The design must bridge the gap between "Clinical Professionalism" and "Luxury Hospitality."

## 2. Target Audience
- Women seeking high-end aesthetic treatments (Facial Lifting, Botox, Skin Cleaning).
- Residents of Blumenau and surrounding regions.
- Clients valuing both clinical results and a sanctuary-like environment for self-care.

## 3. Brand Identity & Tone
- **Personality:** Expert, Aspirational, Warm, and Trustworthy.
- **Tone of Voice:** Sophisticated yet approachable.
- **Key Messaging:** 
  - "Eleve sua beleza, inspire sua essência."
  - "Cuidado que vem de família, resultados que transformam."
  - "Desde 2004."

## 4. Visual Language (Design System)
- **Style:** Modern Editorial / Minimalist Luxury.
- **Color Palette:**
    - Primary: Vibrant Orange (`#fd7a33`) for CTAs and Vitality.
    - Surfaces: Soft Off-White/Beige (`#fff6ec`) for serenity.
    - Accents: Peach and Skin tones (`#f5ae85`, `#fdcfaf`) for warmth.
    - Typography: Deep Bronze/Charcoal for high-legibility without the harshness of pure black.
- **Typography:**
    - Headlines: **Playfair Display** (Serif) for elegance and authority.
    - Body/Functional: **Manrope** (Sans-serif) for modern clarity.
- **Shapes:** 8px rounded corners for a "Soft-Clinical" feel.

## 5. Content Structure & Features
- **Sticky Navigation:** Quick access to Treatments, About, and WhatsApp CTA.
- **Hero Section:** High-impact headline with strategic WhatsApp triggers.
- **Authority Bar:** Highlighting "20+ Years of Experience" and "Blumenau - SC."
- **Storytelling Section:** The legacy of sisters Nubia and Nuzi.
- **Strategic Highlight:** "Tarde de Autocuidado" – focus on private events with friends.
- **Service Grid:** Clean cards for primary treatments (Limpeza de Pele, Botox, etc.).
- **Social Proof:** Testimonials and placeholders for Client Results (Before/After).
- **Location & Contact:** Integrated Google Maps link and direct WhatsApp integration.

## 6. Conversion Strategy (Nielsen Heuristics)
- **Visibility of System Status:** Clear feedback on interactive elements and sticky nav.
- **Aesthetic & Minimalist Design:** Removing clutter to focus on the value proposition.
- **Match between System and Real World:** Using natural, welcoming language.
- **CTAs:** Strategically placed orange buttons (`#fd7a33`) for WhatsApp agendamento: `(47) 99801658`.

## 7. Technical Requirements
- **Responsiveness:** Fluid layout for Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile.
- **Performance:** Optimized images for fast loading.
- **Accessibility:** High contrast ratios for text and intuitive navigation.
